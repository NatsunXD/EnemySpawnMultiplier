Enemy Spawn Multiplier - v16 and v16.6 preview packages

Close the game before upgrading. Remove previous Enemy Spawn Multiplier entries
and install exactly one package:

- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-6x-Native-Composition-v16.zip
- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-6x-Light-Medium-Bias-v16.zip
- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-Preview-Low-Budget-Patrol-Strict-v16.6-preview.zip

Install and enable the official Bingus Shared Loader v15 or newer, deploy with
HDArsenal or HD2MM, and restart the game. Loader v15 discovers this mod from its
declared addon entry; no loader registry edit or custom loader fork is required.
All packages use the same resource ID and cannot be combined.

v16 variants
------------

The two v16 packages keep the Encounter budget override at 6x, raise nonzero
per-type caps and the group clamp to 10x, and divide Patrol/Straggler intervals
by 10. Typical 20-40 second Straggler intervals become 2-4 seconds, 8-14 second
Patrol intervals become 0.8-1.4 seconds, and a group clamp of 10 becomes 100.
The Light-Medium Bias package favors cheaper Encounter templates; Native
Composition keeps native template weights.

v16.6 strict low-budget preview
-------------------------------

The v16.5 preview exposed two separate problems:

- Encounter waves use a separate cooldown deadline at director+0x399D8 in the
  native gate at game.dll+0x94C030. Editing director+0x518B0 does not change
  that deadline.
- A positive cfg+0x78 value bypasses director+0x518B0 in game.dll+0x94AF30.
  Scaling that positive override by 0.1 still leaves a large effective budget.

v16.6 keeps the preview intent but forces the effective cfg+0x78 override to
the scaled director base budget. If the mission starts with director+0x518B0 at
600 and the multiplier is 0.1, both the base budget and the effective override
become 60 instead of leaving a native 21600 override at 2160.

v16.6 still uses:

- Encounter composition budget: 0.1x of the director base value.
- Timed Patrol and Straggler intervals: 0.0-0.1 seconds.
- Nonzero per-type caps and group clamp: 10x.
- GuardForce/static defenders: native budget and native schedule.
- Native template weights, population gates, position queries, queue handling
  and executable code.

The Encounter cooldown deadline is currently logged read-only. It is not written
until its lifecycle and ownership are confirmed across live missions. Writing it
blindly could suppress every later Encounter instead of adding a normal cooldown.

Diagnostics
-----------

After a mission starts, inspect %LOCALAPPDATA%/EnemySpawnMultiplier.log.

- v16: data-v16-native or data-v16-light-medium plus spawn_multiplier_ready.
- v16.6 preview: data-v16.6-preview-low-budget-patrol plus
  spawn_multiplier_ready.

New diagnostic fields:

- o= current cfg+0x78 override value after the mod resolves the active config.
- e= seconds until the read-only Encounter cooldown deadline at 0x399D8.
- m= live Encounter manager list count.

For a strict preview mission with base budget 600, expect p=60.0/600.0,
o=60.00, i=0.00-0.10/0.00-0.10, g=100 and an unchanged gf= value. e= shows
whether the native Encounter gate currently has a pending cooldown; m= shows
whether encounter lists are live. f= identifies the faction from its cap table,
t= reports Patrol/Straggler deadlines shortened on this check, cfg= shows the
selected config source, and l=vanilla confirms executable branches were not
patched.

To uninstall, close the game, disable this mod, purge deployment, and redeploy
the remaining mods.
