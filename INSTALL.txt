Enemy Spawn Multiplier 6x - v17 variants

Close the game before upgrading. Remove previous Enemy Spawn Multiplier entries
and install exactly one package:

- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-6x-Native-Composition-v17.zip
- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-6x-Light-Medium-Bias-v17.zip

Install and enable the official Bingus Shared Loader v15 or newer, deploy with
HDArsenal or HD2MM, and restart the game. Loader v15 discovers this mod from its
declared addon entry; no loader registry edit or custom fork is required. Both
variants use the same resource ID and cannot be combined.

v17 keeps the Encounter budget override at 6x, raises nonzero per-type caps and
the group clamp to 10x, and divides Patrol/Straggler intervals by 10. Typical
20-40 second Straggler intervals become 2-4 seconds, 8-14 second Patrol
intervals become 0.8-1.4 seconds, and a group clamp of 10 becomes 100.

The module also checks the two pending Patrol/Straggler deadlines every 100 ms.
A future deadline longer than its new maximum interval is shortened to that
maximum. Deadlines already due or within the configured interval are untouched.
This covers the initial native delay and the native 5-second retry delay after a
failed Patrol position query without repeatedly dividing the timer.

The desired target at cfg+0x50 remains native. v13 doubled this value, but the
same value participates in the native combined-100 rejection formula and could
reduce spawning in some population states. Pending queue quantities, population
counters and executable code remain unchanged. Every approved Encounter or
Patrol queue request is re-enqueued once through the game's native queue
function, for two scheduled requests in total.

The Light-Medium Bias variant ranks supported Encounter templates by cost per
planned unit. If a faction exposes an incompatible template layout, such as the
currently observed Automaton pool, v17 keeps native weights and continues the
core budget, cap and interval tuning instead of stopping midway.

Illuminate missions are identified by their observed 45-row faction cap table. Their
GuardForce budget is reduced to one quarter during initialization so static
defenders leave room under the shared native population gate. Existing entities
and live population counters are never rewritten.

After a mission starts, inspect %LOCALAPPDATA%/EnemySpawnMultiplier.log.
native-queue-v17-native or native-queue-v17-light-medium plus
spawn_multiplier_ready means the
active config was resolved. Expected values for the common 20-40 / 8-14 / 10
config are i=2.00-4.00/0.80-1.40 g=100. f= identifies the faction from its cap
table, gf= shows current/original GuardForce budget, and w= reports bias status.
t= reports shortened deadlines. cfg= shows the selected config source. q= shows
replayed/observed eligible requests. l=vanilla confirms the native population
branches were not patched.

The native 70/448 population gates, position queries and queue processing still
apply to original requests. v17 also duplicates each already-approved
Encounter and Patrol request through the native queue without patching those
executable-code gates.

To uninstall, close the game, disable this mod, purge deployment, and redeploy
the remaining mods.
