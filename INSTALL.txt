Enemy Spawn Multiplier - v16 and v16.7 preview packages

Close the game before upgrading. Remove previous Enemy Spawn Multiplier entries
and install exactly one package:

- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-6x-Native-Composition-v16.zip
- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-6x-Light-Medium-Bias-v16.zip
- Enemy-Spawn-Multiplier-Preview-Low-Budget-Fast-Cadence-v16.7-preview.zip

Install and enable the official Bingus Shared Loader v15 or newer, deploy with
HDArsenal or HD2MM, and restart the game. Loader v15 discovers this mod from its
declared addon entry; no loader registry edit or custom loader fork is required.
All packages use the same resource ID and cannot be combined.

v16 variants
------------

The two v16 packages keep the Encounter budget override at 6x, raise nonzero
per-type caps and the group clamp to 10x, and divide Patrol/Straggler intervals
by 10. The Light-Medium Bias package favors cheaper Encounter templates; Native
Composition keeps native template weights.

v16.7 fast-cadence preview
--------------------------

v16.6 proved that the Encounter budget is the reinforcement budget: at 0.1x the
waves became smaller and the next wave arrived sooner. Two native timers were
still untouched:

- Enemy reinforcement cooldown: written by game.dll+0x94CCE8 into
  director+0x399D8 and read by the admission gate at game.dll+0x94C030.
- Patrol/Straggler refresh: cfg+0x38/+0x3C and cfg+0x40/+0x44, which the mod
  already writes.

v16.7 therefore:

- Forces the effective cfg+0x78 override to the scaled director base so the
  0.1x budget cannot be bypassed by a positive native override.
- Clamps the enemy reinforcement cooldown at director+0x399D8 to two seconds
  whenever it is further away, using the same checked, readback, idempotent
  write path as the Patrol/Straggler deadlines.
- Keeps timed Patrol/Straggler intervals at 0.0-0.1 seconds with the 10x group
  clamp.
- Leaves GuardForce/static defenders on the native budget and schedule.
- Adds read-only scheduler diagnostics: the reinforcement cooldown delta, the
  live Encounter list count, the two scheduling counters A and B that gate the
  whole timed scheduler, the Patrol/Straggler deadline deltas, and the four
  scheduler enable flags.

The native timed scheduler exits entirely when A is at or above the desired
target, or when (target-A)+(B-A) reaches 100. Those values are now visible in
the log so a weak Patrol effect can be attributed to the gate instead of the
interval.

Diagnostics
-----------

After a mission starts, inspect %LOCALAPPDATA%/EnemySpawnMultiplier.log.

- v16: data-v16-native or data-v16-light-medium plus spawn_multiplier_ready.
- v16.7 preview: data-v16.7-preview-low-budget-patrol plus
  spawn_multiplier_ready.

Diagnostic fields:

- o=  effective cfg+0x78 override after the mod resolves the active config.
- e=  seconds until the enemy reinforcement cooldown at 0x399D8 (negative means
      already due).
- m=  live Encounter manager list count.
- a=  scheduling counter A (manager+0x4A4) used by the A >= target exit.
- b=  scheduling counter B (manager+0x1C) used by the combined-100 exit.
- pd= Patrol deadline delta in seconds.
- sd= Straggler deadline delta in seconds.
- fl= the four scheduler enable flags at 0x5189C/0x518A0/0x518A4/0x518A8.
- t=  deadlines shortened on this check.
- n=  a deadline existed but its owning field was not writable private data.

For the preview, expect p=60.0/600.0, o=60.00, i=0.00-0.10/0.00-0.10, g=100,
an unchanged gf= value, and e= at or below 2.00 shortly after a wave.

To uninstall, close the game, disable this mod, purge deployment, and redeploy
the remaining mods.
